Cadence

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information Cadence collects and how it is used. We collect only what is needed to provide the service. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Information we collect

You can use Cadence without an account. In that case your library and settings are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture.
  • Library data: your liked songs, playlists, saved albums and artists, and listening history. For each song you play, the history holds the song, when and for how long you listened, what you played it from, whether you finished or skipped it, the kind of device, and that device's time zone offset.
  • Profile and friends: the name and picture on your profile, your friend code and the accounts you follow.
  • Music service: if you connect a music service account, the name and email address of that account, so that your other devices can show which one you connected. The connection itself stays on your device and is not sent to us.

With or without an account, the app sends anonymous usage statistics while it is in use: a random identifier created on your device, the app's version and platform, whether an account and a music service account are connected, and counts of songs played and of errors. We record the country a report comes from, and not the IP address. These statistics are not linked to your account and say nothing about what you listen to.

How we use information

We use this information to sync your library across your devices, to keep your account secure and to operate the service. We use the usage statistics to see how many copies of the app are in use and on which versions, and to find faults. Cadence contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with service providers that host our systems and deliver sign-in emails, and only as needed to provide the service, or where the law requires it.

If you use friends, accounts that follow you can see your profile name and picture. The song you last played is shown to them only while you have turned that on in your profile. You can turn it off or remove a follower at any time.

Third-party services

Cadence connects to third-party services to deliver music, artwork and lyrics. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply.

Storage and security

Account and library data are stored on servers in the European Union. Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted, and each account can access only its own data and what other accounts have chosen to share with it.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all data associated with it at any time from Settings in the app. Signing out stops syncing and leaves your library on your device. Usage statistics are not tied to an account, so they are not removed with one.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. You can exercise these rights from your account settings in the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
