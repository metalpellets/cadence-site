Cadence

Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

The short version

Cadence stores what it needs to keep your library in sync between your devices, and nothing else. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

If you do not sign in

Your library stays on your device. Nothing about you is sent to Cadence.

If you sign in with Google

Google tells Cadence your name, your email address, your profile picture and an identifier for your Google account. Cadence never sees your Google password, and it gets no access to your mail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

Cadence stores those details together with your library:

  • your liked songs and your playlists
  • the albums, artists and playlists you saved
  • what you played recently
  • your listening history: which songs you played, when and for how long

Where it is kept

In a database hosted by Supabase in the European Union. Each account can reach only its own data. The person who runs Cadence administers that database and so is technically able to read it, but does not look through, share or sell anyone's data.

Other services the app talks to

To find and play music and to show artwork and lyrics, the app connects straight from your device to the online services that provide them. Those services receive the requests, including your device's network address, under their own privacy policies. If you connect an account at such a service inside the app, that sign-in is kept on your device and is not sent to Cadence.

Deleting your data

Signing out stops the syncing and leaves your library on your device. To have your account and everything stored with it removed from the server, ask the person who gave you Cadence, and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
